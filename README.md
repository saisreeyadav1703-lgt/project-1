# Rooftop Rainwater Harvesting and Artificial Recharge
This project focuses on designing an efficient Rooftop Rainwater Harvesting (RWH) system and exploring methods for Artificial Groundwater Recharge. The aim is to conserve water, reduce dependency on external water sources, and promote sustainable groundwater restoration.
## 📘 Project Overview
Rooftop Rainwater Harvesting collects rainwater falling on the roof surface, filters it, and stores it in a tank or directs it into the ground through recharge pits.  
Artificial Recharge techniques help increase groundwater levels using percolation pits, trenches, bore recharge, and filtration systems.
## 🛠️ Objectives
- Conserve rainwater and reduce wastage  
- Improve groundwater levels through artificial recharge  
- Design an efficient and cost-effective system  
- Promote environmental sustainability  
## 🌧️ How the System Works
1. *Collection:* Rainwater is collected from the rooftop through gutters.  
2. *Filtration:* Water passes through a mesh filter or sand/charcoal filter.  
3. *Storage:* Water is collected in tanks for domestic/non-potable use.  
4. *Recharge:* Excess water is diverted into the ground via recharge pits or wells.
## 🧪 Key Components
- Catchment area (rooftop)  
- Pipes and gutters  
- First flush device  
- Filtration unit  
- Storage tank  
- Recharge pit or recharge well  
## 🌍 Benefits
- Reduces water scarcity  
- Improves groundwater levels  
- Reduces soil erosion and flooding  
- Low cost and eco-friendly  
- Useful for households, schools, industries, and apartments
